# Christopher MacMaster

I'm a data engineer with a background in political science and public opinion research. I spent three years at Civera building Python ETL pipelines and data-quality checks for election data, after starting my career programming, fielding and analyzing surveys as a polling analyst. My hobbies include online games, watching basketball, and enjoying time outdoors. 😁

* Currently building an open hub for U.S. public opinion data: a Dagster pipeline that collects polls and survey data (FiveThirtyEight's archive, Pew Research Center, the General Social Survey, the American National Election Studies, the Cooperative Election Study, and university pollsters like Quinnipiac and Siena), checks its quality, and standardizes it for search and analysis
* Open to data engineering roles (remote, U.S.)

You can reach me at: [christopher.macmaster@gmail.com](mailto:christopher.macmaster@gmail.com)
